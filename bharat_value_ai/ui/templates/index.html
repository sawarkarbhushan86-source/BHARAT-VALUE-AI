📐 Advanced Institutional Valuation Methodology & Mathematical Formulations
Click to Expand

A. Non-Financial Industrial DCF (FCFF)

• Discount Rate (WACC): Weighted Average Cost of Capital blending cost of equity (Ke via CAPM: Rf 6.8% + Beta × ERP 5.7%) and post-tax cost of debt.

• Terminal Value (Gordon Growth): $TV = \frac{FCF_n \times (1 + g)}{WACC - g}$, mathematically constrained to $WACC > g$.

• Enterprise Value Bridge: Explicitly subtracts audited non-current borrowings and adds cash reserves.

B. Banking & NBFC Valuation (RIM & Justified P/B)

• Why DCF Fails for Banks: Customer deposits are operating liabilities, not industrial debt. Capex and working capital do not reflect banking risk assets.

• Residual Income Model: $V_0 = BV_0 + \sum_{t=1}^n \frac{(ROE_t - Ke) \times BV_{t-1}}{(1 + Ke)^t} + TV_{RI}$. Excess return generated over cost of equity.

• Justified P/B: $P/B = \frac{ROE - g}{Ke - g}$. Banks earning above Ke trade at a justified premium to book value.
